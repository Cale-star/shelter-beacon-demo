The wiring.

Every switch runs from a pin to ground. The board's own pull-up resistors do the rest, so there are no resistors to add except the one for the light.

PartBoard pinOther side
Water switchGPIO 6GND
Medical switchGPIO 33GND
Food switchGPIO 47GND
Blankets switchGPIO 4GND
Fuel switchGPIO 48GND
People knobGPIO 3, middle pinOuter pins to 3V3 and GND
Status lightGPIO 38, through 100 ΩGND
BeeperGPIO 39, the S pin+ to 3V3, − to GND

Leave GPIO 2, 5 and 7 alone. On the V4.3 they run the radio's amplifier. The first V4 build put switches there and the radio fought them.

Power the beeper from 3.3 V, not 5 V. If it beeps without stopping, your module triggers on low: set BUZZER_ACTIVE_LOW to 1 in the firmware.

In the Arduino IDE choose "ESP32S3 Dev Module" and turn on USB CDC On Boot, or the serial monitor stays blank.

Putting it together.

  1. Print the template and drill the lid.Tape page 1 of the overlay PDF to the lid, centre-punch every hole, and step-drill slowly. Check the light holder's thread before drilling its hole.
  2. Mount the overlay.Laminate the face, or have a sign shop print it on matte vinyl. Punch the holes and stick it down with transfer tape.
  3. Fit the controls.Switches, knob, light and beeper go through the lid. Fit the printed knob with its set screw.
  4. Wire the lid.Follow the table above. Label every wire before closing anything.
  5. Build the power side.Battery holder and charger on the mounting plate, then the solar panel's lead to the charger and the charger's output to the board.
  6. Flash both boards.Box v0.6 on the V4.3, receiver v0.4-ack on the V3. The box chirps twice on power-up.
  7. Test before you close it.Open the dashboard, connect the receiver, flip each switch and turn the knob through every click. Every change should give two quick beeps.

How the firmware got here.

  1. v0.6November 6
    The box waits for an answer.

    Headquarters replies to every report. Two quick beeps mean it arrived; one long beep means the box is still trying, and it keeps trying by itself after 5, 15 and 30 seconds, then every minute. Needs receiver v0.4-ack. Not yet tested on the real boards.

  2. v0.5November 6
    A knob, a light and a beeper.

    One 11-click knob replaces the two thumbwheels. A front status light and a beeper give the person feedback without a screen.

  3. v0.4November 2
    Moved to the V4.3 board.

    Three switches moved off pins the new board's amplifier needs. A range-test build also sends a report every 5 seconds so a walk logs plenty of data.

  4. v0.3October 20
    Quiet until something changes.

    The box stopped sending every two seconds and only speaks when a switch moves, which saves battery and airtime. Five needs and a people count in one report.

  5. v0.2Before the range test
    A light you can see.

    The board's LED blinks on every send and stays on while a need is switched on.

  6. v0.1The start
    Two switches over radio.

    Water and medical, sent every two seconds as PKT,n,W1,M0.

Every version is still in the firmware folder.
