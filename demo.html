Flip a switch. Watch headquarters.

After an earthquake takes out phones and power, a shelter leader flips a switch for each thing the shelter needs. The box radios it to headquarters. No cell service, no internet, no app.

Go ahead and flip a switch. To change the number of people, drag the knob or tap a number.

    How it works

    Every change is sent up to three times. Headquarters confirms the first copy it hears, and the box beeps twice so the leader knows it arrived. If nothing comes back, the box keeps trying on its own.

    Each shelter gets a score: medical 3, water 2, and 1 each for food, blankets and fuel. A crowded shelter adds 1 at 25 people and 2 at 50. A score of 3 or more is high priority and sets off the alarm.
